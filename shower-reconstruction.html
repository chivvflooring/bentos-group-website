A bathtub-to-walk-in-shower conversion or a full bathroom renovation?

A tub-to-shower conversion can focus on the existing bathtub area without automatically becoming a full bathroom remodel. Share the listing or room photos, approximate tub width if available, your city and when the home will be accessible. What we review in the written scope

Existing shower and removal

Describe previous repairs and the areas you want assessed. Confirm protection, demolition, debris removal and how concealed conditions will be handled before work begins.

Base, waterproofing and tile

Discuss the drain, shower base, waterproofing system, wall connections, curb or entry, tile layout and grout. The proposal should identify the preparation and testing included, rather than describe tile installation alone.

Fixtures, glass and finishes

Identify valves, shower fittings, niche, bench and glass as separate scope decisions. Confirm allowances, owner-supplied products, exclusions and related licensed trade work.

Questions about tub-to-shower conversions and rebuilding a shower

Can you replace a bathtub with a walk-in shower?

Yes, the existing bathtub area can be evaluated for a walk-in shower. The final scope depends on the opening, plumbing and drain location, subfloor and wall conditions, shower base, waterproofing, finish material, fixtures and glass. Listing photos can start the conversation, but measurements and access are needed before a final estimate.

Can only the shower floor be rebuilt?

A focused reconstruction may be considered after reviewing the existing assembly and the connections to the walls, drain and curb. Photos alone do not establish what can be retained. We discuss what evaluation or opening-up is needed before defining a repair scope.

Do new grout or caulk resolve the whole problem?

Surface maintenance and reconstruction are different scopes. Tell us what you are seeing and what has already been attempted so the underlying condition can be assessed before a solution is proposed.

What affects shower reconstruction cost?

Removal, access, existing conditions, base and drain work, waterproofing, tile pattern, fixtures and glass affect the estimate. Confirm quantities, allowances, inclusions and exclusions in writing; there is no fixed price for every shower.

Do I need measurements and finish selections first?

No. Start with your name, city and preferred contact method. Photos and approximate dimensions help during follow-up; site measurements and product choices can follow the initial discussion.

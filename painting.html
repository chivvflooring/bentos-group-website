Interior painting

Tell us which rooms and surfaces need painting: walls, ceilings, trim, doors or cabinetry. These are separate scope choices, so your estimate can clearly state what is included. Share any drywall repairs, stains or existing finish issues during follow-up.

Exterior painting

We review siding, trim, doors and other requested surfaces, along with cleaning, surface preparation and sealing needs. Deteriorated materials or repairs are discussed separately. Weather and surface conditions affect scheduling.

Preparation, protection and finish

Your written scope should identify preparation, protection of nearby surfaces, included repairs, paint products and cleanup. Colors and sheen are confirmed before materials are purchased. Tell us if the home will be occupied during the work.

Questions before you start

Are ceilings and trim automatically included?

No. List the surfaces you want painted so the estimate can include them explicitly.

Can painting be combined with flooring or remodeling?

Yes. Include the related work in your request so we can discuss sequencing and a coordinated scope.

Discuss your project

Serving Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Milton, Roswell and Cumming. Include your city or ZIP code so we can confirm project coverage.

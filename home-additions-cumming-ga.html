FAQ

Home Addition Questions

What types of home additions can you build?

Home additions can include bedrooms, bathrooms, living spaces, home offices and other custom spaces depending on the property, design and project scope.

Is a home addition different from a home renovation?

Yes. A renovation generally remodels existing space, while a home addition expands the home's existing footprint by creating new space.

Can you add a bedroom to an existing home?

A bedroom addition can be considered when the property, design, zoning requirements and project scope allow for an expansion.

Can you add a bathroom to an existing home?

Yes. A bathroom can be incorporated into a new addition when the home's layout, plumbing requirements and project conditions support the design.

How much does a home addition cost in Cumming, GA?

The cost of a home addition varies significantly based on the size, location, structural work, utilities, finishes and complexity of the project. A project-specific evaluation is needed for a meaningful estimate.

How long does a home addition take?

The timeline depends on the size and complexity of the addition, design requirements, materials, construction work and project approvals.

Can a home addition include a kitchen or bathroom?

Yes. An addition can be designed around a kitchen, bathroom or other functional space when the property's layout and project requirements make it appropriate.

How do I start a home addition project?

Contact Bento's Group with information about your home, the additional space you need and how you want to use it. The project can then be discussed and evaluated around your property's requirements.

Deck project questions

Can you replace rotted deck boards?

We review the damaged area and surrounding conditions before defining the repair. Visible surface damage may require a closer look at the supports and connections.

Do you work on stairs, railings and covered porches?

Yes. Send photos or plans with your city and goals so we can discuss those elements as part of the project scope.

Can every wood deck be tiled?

No. A tiled exterior surface needs a suitable structure and an appropriate exterior assembly, drainage and waterproofing approach. The featured project is an example of a finish, not a recommendation to tile an existing deck without assessment.

How much will my deck project cost?

Size, condition, height, access, demolition, material selection and structural work affect the price. Share photos and your goals to start a project-specific estimate.

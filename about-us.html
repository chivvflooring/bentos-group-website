    Bento's Group: Residential Construction • Remodeling • Project Development in Alpharetta, GA.

    The Evolution of a Brand

    At Bento's Group, we believe that a home is more than just a structure — it is a sanctuary. For projects large and small, our team has been dedicated to enhancing the living spaces of Georgia residents with meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to longevity.

    Bento's Group and CHIVV Flooring provide one customer-facing path for specialty hardwood and tile work, painting, remodeling and residential project coordination across North Atlanta.

    Bento's Group is not a licensed general contractor. When an agreed scope requires a licensed general contractor, licensed trade, design professional or permit responsibility, the appropriate qualified partner and each party's role are identified for that project.

    Why North Georgia Chooses Bento's Group

    What sets us apart in the competitive Alpharetta remodeling market is our white-glove approach. We prioritize clean jobsites, transparent communication, and strict adherence to timelines.

    • Project Experience: Practical planning and coordination across residential projects.
    • Scope-Based Compliance: Permitting and licensed professional participation are structured for each project’s requirements.
    • Master Craftsmanship: Specialized skills in high-end materials and luxury finishes.
    • Local Accountability: Proudly serving our neighbors in Alpharetta, Milton, and Roswell.

    Our Specialized Services

    Kitchen & Bathroom Remodeling

    We specialize in turning outdated spaces into modern masterpieces. From custom cabinetry and quartz countertops in Roswell to spa-like bathroom retreats in Milton, our designs increase both lifestyle quality and property value.

    The Flooring Authority

    Building on our work as Chivv Flooring, we offer hardwood installation and refinishing, engineered wood and tile flooring in North Georgia. Epoxy, terrazzo and vinyl flooring are available for commercial projects. Explore flooring options and request an estimate.

    Our expertise extends beyond the interior. We design and install durable outdoor patios, pavers, and decks that withstand the Georgia climate while providing the perfect backdrop for entertainment.

    Bento's Group: Residential Construction • Remodeling • Project Development in Alpharetta, GA.

    The Evolution of a Brand

    At Bento's Group, we believe that a home is more than just a structure — it is a sanctuary. For projects large and small, our team has been dedicated to enhancing the living spaces of Georgia residents with meticulous attention to detail and a commitment to longevity.

    Founded as Chivv Flooring, we built our reputation through specialty hardwood and tile work in North Georgia. As our clients' needs grew, so did we. Today, we have evolved into a residential construction, remodeling, and project development company, offering comprehensive solutions for every corner of your home.

    "Our transition from Chivv Flooring to Bento's Group reflects our expanded capability to handle complex residential renovations while maintaining the specialized precision we were founded on."

    — Charles Silva, Founder

    Why North Georgia Chooses Bento's Group

    What sets us apart in the competitive Alpharetta remodeling market is our white-glove approach. We prioritize clean jobsites, transparent communication, and strict adherence to timelines.

    • Project Experience: Practical planning and coordination across residential projects.
    • Scope-Based Compliance: Permitting and licensed professional participation are structured for each project’s requirements.
    • Master Craftsmanship: Specialized skills in high-end materials and luxury finishes.
    • Local Accountability: Proudly serving our neighbors in Alpharetta, Milton, and Roswell.

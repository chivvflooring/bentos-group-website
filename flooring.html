Should you refinish hardwood or replace your flooring?

If you like your existing wood floor but its finish is worn, start by asking whether refinishing is possible. Solid hardwood and some engineered floors can be candidates, depending on the remaining wood above the tongue or wear-layer thickness, past sanding and current condition. A site review is needed before choosing the scope.

Can sanding remove dark stains or water damage?

Some marks affect only the finish; others extend into the wood. Sanding cannot be promised to remove deep stains, and damaged boards may need repair or replacement. Tell us about leaks, pet damage or movement and send close-up photos. Moisture sources need to be addressed before the floor is repaired.

What affects the cost of hardwood refinishing or installation?

For refinishing, the rooms, existing finish, repairs, stairs, furniture and access affect the scope. For a new floor, removal, subfloor preparation, the selected product, transitions and molding also matter. Your written estimate should identify the work and exclusions; a square-foot rate alone does not describe the entire project.

Should I buy flooring before requesting an estimate?

You can request an estimate before buying. If a product is already selected, share its name and installation instructions so we can review the installation method, quantities and preparation. Let us confirm the scope before committing to a purchase for an unmeasured project.

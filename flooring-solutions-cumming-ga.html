Flooring That Complements the Entire Home

Flooring has a major effect on how a room looks, feels and connects with the rest of the house. When floors are worn, outdated or simply no longer fit the space, replacing them can completely change the character of an interior.

Bento's Group provides flooring solutions as both standalone projects and as part of larger remodeling and renovation work. The goal is not simply to install a new surface, but to make the flooring work with the room, the home's design and the overall renovation.

Flooring Options

Solutions for Different Spaces

The right flooring depends on the room, the way it is used and the look you want to achieve.

Hardwood Flooring

A timeless option for homeowners looking for a natural appearance, warmth and a finish that can complement a wide range of interior styles.

Luxury Vinyl

A practical choice for homeowners looking for attractive flooring with durability and versatility across frequently used areas.

Tile Flooring

Tile can provide a durable and visually flexible surface for kitchens, bathrooms, entryways and other areas of the home.

Laminate Flooring

Laminate offers a wide range of styles and can be considered when updating living spaces and other interior areas.

Room-to-Room Flooring

Coordinate flooring across connected spaces to create a more consistent appearance throughout the home.

Whole-Home Flooring

Replace flooring throughout multiple areas as part of a larger home renovation or remodeling project.

FAQ

Flooring Questions

What flooring options are available for a home renovation?

Flooring options depend on the room, desired appearance, durability requirements and project scope. Different materials can be considered for kitchens, bathrooms, living areas, bedrooms and other spaces.

Can flooring be replaced throughout an entire home?

Yes. Flooring replacement can be planned across multiple rooms or an entire home, allowing materials and transitions to be coordinated throughout the property.

Can you install flooring as part of a larger remodeling project?

Yes. Flooring installation can be incorporated into kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, full-home renovations and other residential construction projects.

How much does flooring installation cost in Cumming?

Flooring installation costs depend on the material, square footage, existing floor condition, preparation requirements, room layout and installation complexity. A project-specific estimate is more accurate than a generic price.

How long does flooring installation take?

Installation time depends on the size of the project, flooring material, preparation work and number of rooms involved. Larger whole-home projects naturally require more planning and installation time.

Do you provide flooring for kitchens and bathrooms?

Yes. Flooring can be included in kitchen and bathroom remodeling projects as well as broader home renovation work.

How do I get started with a flooring project?

Contact Bento's Group with information about the rooms you want to update and the type of flooring project you have in mind. The project can then be discussed and scoped around your home.

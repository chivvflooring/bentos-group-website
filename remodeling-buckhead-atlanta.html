Questions worth asking before choosing a remodeler

How should I compare contractor reviews?

Read the current rating, number of reviews and the experiences behind them. Look for the type of work you need, communication and how concerns were handled. A star rating is a starting point; ask about the proposed scope, project responsibilities and relevant project examples too.

Are your photos completed projects or design concepts?

Our website labels design visualizations and retouched images. Original progress photos can show unfinished details. Read each caption and ask about examples relevant to your project; a design concept is not a photograph of completed work.

What changes the renovation budget?

Room size alone is not enough. Demolition, preparation, cabinetry and material selections, fixture relocation, repairs discovered during work, protection and access affect the estimate. Confirm allowances, exclusions, material purchasing and change-order terms before work starts.

What should I send for the first conversation?

Your city or ZIP, the rooms involved, a preferred contact method and a short description are enough to begin. Photos, approximate dimensions, budget range and desired timing help us assess fit. Measurements and a site appointment can follow.

Can flooring be coordinated with a kitchen or bathroom?

Yes. Discuss cabinets, floor heights, doorways and transitions together so the work sequence and surface protection can be planned. Required licensed trades, permits and structural review depend on the agreed scope.

Do you have a Buckhead office?

We are based in Alpharetta and serve Buckhead by project appointment. Send the property location so we can confirm coverage and scheduling.

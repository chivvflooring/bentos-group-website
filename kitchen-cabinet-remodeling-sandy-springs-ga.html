Questions from Sandy Springs homeowners and buyers

Can I update the cabinets without remodeling the whole kitchen?

Yes. If the cabinet boxes and layout remain suitable, a focused cabinet scope can be discussed separately from counters, flooring, lighting and other work. Existing materials and finishes must be reviewed before the scope is agreed.

What information should I send for a kitchen estimate?

Send the project city, property status, access date, wide room photos, cabinet and appliance photos, the items you want to keep, and the changes you want to make. Measurements and selections can follow the initial conversation.

Who handles plumbing, electrical and structural work?

The written project scope identifies the responsible parties. Work requiring a licensed professional or structural review is assigned to the appropriate licensed partner or design professional for the agreed project.

Can flooring and painting be included?

Yes. Include hardwood refinishing or replacement, wall and ceiling painting, trim and cabinet finishing in the request so protection and work sequence can be discussed together.

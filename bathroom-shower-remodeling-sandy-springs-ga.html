Choose the right scope

A shower conversion and a complete bathroom are different projects

A focused tub-to-shower conversion may stay within the existing bathing area. A full renovation may include the vanity, countertop, bathroom floor, lighting, ventilation, painting, storage and layout changes. List every item you expect to change so the written scope can separate the work clearly.

Bento’s Group reviews the renovation scope, materials and trade coordination. Plumbing and electrical work that requires a licensed professional is assigned to the appropriate licensed partner; design, permitting and contract responsibilities are established for the agreed project.

Use the project photo to identify features, then price your actual room

Point out the features you want: open glass, niche, bench, tub removal, vanity configuration, tile size, lighting or storage. Photos are useful for the first conversation, while measurements and existing conditions determine the project-specific scope.

Information that makes the first call useful

  1. Property status: already owned, under contract or still considering a purchase.
  2. Access: when a site review is possible and whether the home is occupied.
  3. Scope: shower or tub, vanity, floor, walls, lighting, painting and any layout change.
  4. Photos: one wide room view plus the shower, plumbing wall, vanity and floor.
  5. Timing: a desired completion or move-in date, reviewed before any schedule is confirmed.

Questions from Sandy Springs homeowners and buyers

Can a bathtub be converted to a walk-in shower?

Yes. The existing opening, plumbing and drain location, subfloor, walls, waterproofing, finish material, fixtures and glass are reviewed before the scope and price are confirmed.

Can listing photos be used before I have access to the home?

Listing photos can start the conversation, but they do not confirm measurements, concealed conditions or a final price. Share the property status and expected access date so the appropriate next step can be discussed.

Who handles plumbing and electrical work?

The written scope identifies the responsible parties. Plumbing and electrical work that requires a licensed professional is assigned to the appropriate licensed partner for the agreed project.

What should be included in a bathroom estimate request?

Include your city, property status, access date, the fixtures and surfaces you want changed, photos and your preferred contact method. Mention any known water damage or previous repairs so they can be reviewed.

    Professional Construction & Remodeling Services in Alpharetta and Nearby

    From custom new builds to expert home maintenance, Bento's Group delivers high-end craftsmanship across North Georgia.

    New Construction & Structural Additions

    New Construction

    Residential construction projects require careful planning and coordinated execution. For new construction in Alpharetta, we coordinate planning, materials, skilled trades, and construction management; permitting and licensed professional participation are structured according to scope and applicable requirements.

    Home Additions

    Need more space? Whether it’s a second-story addition, a new sunroom, or a guest suite, we seamlessly integrate home additions into your existing structure, increasing both square footage and property value.

    Full Home Renovation

    Transform your entire living environment. Our full home renovation services breathe new life into older properties, updating layouts, systems, and aesthetics to meet modern standards of luxury and comfort.

    Interior Remodeling & Specialty Services

    Kitchen Remodeling

    The heart of your home deserves a masterpiece. We specialize in custom kitchen remodeling in Roswell and Milton, focusing on functional layouts, high-end cabinetry, and premium quartz or granite countertops.

    Bathroom Remodeling

    Turn your bathroom into a spa-like retreat. From walk-in glass showers and freestanding tubs to intricate tile work, our bathroom renovations prioritize relaxation and modern design.

    Flooring Solutions

    Continuing the legacy of Chivv Flooring, we are the North Georgia authority on hardwood refinishing, luxury vinyl plank (LVP) installation, and custom tile patterns for any room.

    Interior & Exterior Painting

    A professional paint job is the final touch of any renovation. We provide premium painting services using high-quality, durable finishes that withstand the Georgia climate and daily wear.

    Outdoor Living & Property Upkeep

    Pavers & Hardscaping

What affects a hardwood refinishing estimate?

Room sizes are one part of the scope. The existing finish, floor condition, closets, stairs, furniture handling, access and the selected finish system also need to be discussed. A whole open room and several small rooms can involve different preparation and edge work.

Ask for a written breakdown of the work, material or finish allowances, exclusions and payment terms. We review your project before giving a price; photos help start the conversation but do not replace a site assessment.

Plan your refinishing project

Can my engineered hardwood be refinished?

It depends on the product’s remaining wear layer, previous sanding and condition. If you know the manufacturer or product name, share it with us. We need to review the floor before recommending sanding; replacement may be the appropriate option.

Will sanding remove every stain or scratch?

No result can be promised from a photo alone. Marks can be limited to the finish or extend into the wood. Show us the areas that concern you so the assessment and written scope reflect those conditions.

How long will I be out of the rooms?

The project area, preparation and finish system determine the schedule. Ask separately about walking on the floor, moving furniture back and replacing rugs. Follow the selected finish manufacturer’s drying and curing instructions; these are not all the same milestone.

What about sanding dust, furniture and pets?

Discuss the equipment and containment plan, which rooms need to be cleared, and how the work area will be separated from the rest of the home. Confirm who moves furniture and how children and pets will be kept out of the work area before the start date.

Can I choose the stain and sheen?

Tell us whether you prefer a natural look, a different stain or a change in sheen. Discuss product choices and samples in your home before the finish is selected. Do not assume that a color in a photograph will look identical on your wood.

Where do you work?

Our flooring service area includes Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Milton, Roswell and Cumming. Include your city or ZIP code when you get in touch so we can confirm coverage and arrange the next step.

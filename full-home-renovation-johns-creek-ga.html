    FAQ

    Full Home Renovation Questions

    What is included in a full home renovation?

    A full home renovation can involve multiple areas of a residence, including kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, painting, trim, layouts, finishes and other interior improvements depending on the property's needs and project scope.

    Can you renovate multiple rooms at the same time?

    Yes. A whole-home renovation can coordinate work across multiple rooms so that materials, finishes, construction and scheduling are considered as one larger project.

    Can an older home be completely renovated?

    Yes. Older homes can be renovated to improve layouts, finishes, functionality and selected building systems while preserving features that homeowners want to keep.

    How much does a full home renovation cost in Johns Creek?

    The cost depends heavily on the home's size, existing condition, materials, number of rooms, structural changes and systems involved. A project-specific assessment is more useful than a generic price range.

    Can you remodel only part of an existing home?

    Yes. A renovation does not necessarily have to involve every room. The project can be scoped around the areas that provide the greatest improvement to the home.

    How do I start a whole-home renovation?

    Contact Bento's Group with information about the property, the areas you want to renovate and your goals for the project. The next step is to discuss the scope and determine the appropriate approach.

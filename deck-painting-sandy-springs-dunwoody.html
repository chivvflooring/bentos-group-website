Questions before planning the work

Can you paint or stain over an old deck finish?

That depends on the existing finish, its condition and the proposed product. We review the surface before defining cleaning, coating removal or other preparation. Coating over a failing finish is not a repair for the underlying problem.

Can you replace rotted exterior trim or deck boards?

Send photos so we can discuss the visible damage. The extent of deterioration may only become clear after closer inspection or removal. Material replacement, structural work and painting are identified separately in the agreed scope.

Can neighbors request estimates together?

Yes. Each homeowner can send a separate request with their city, surfaces and timing. We can discuss coordinating appointments where practical; each property receives its own scope and price. Sending a request does not guarantee a group discount or reserve a work date.

What if my subdivision requires color approval?

Tell us about HOA requirements and confirm the approved colors before paint is ordered. Approval responsibilities and documentation are discussed with the homeowner.

What affects the estimate?

Surface area, height, access, current coatings, repairs, furniture protection, materials and preparation all matter. Your estimate should identify included surfaces, products, repairs, exclusions and cleanup. Exterior scheduling depends on weather and suitable surface conditions.

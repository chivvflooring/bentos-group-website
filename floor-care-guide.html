How to keep your wood floors clean and beautiful

The best cleaning routine starts with the finish on your floor. Odie's Oil and Rubio Monocoat are different systems: use the cleaner recommended for the finish you actually have.

First identify the finish. If you do not know whether your floor is Odie's, Rubio, polyurethane, or another product, check your installation paperwork or ask the installer before using a specialty cleaner or applying oil. Test a small, inconspicuous area first.

The simple everyday routine

  1. Sweep or vacuum grit with a floor-safe soft attachment; grit can scratch the surface.
  2. Wipe spills promptly. For routine cleaning, use a lightly damp microfiber cloth or mop rather than leaving standing water on the boards.
  3. Put mats at entrances and pads under furniture, and follow the finish maker's cure-time guidance before wet cleaning a newly finished floor.

Floors finished with Odie's Oil

For Odie's treated wood, the maker describes mixing 2% Odie's cleaning concentrate with 98% water in a spray bottle for a wipe-and-dry cleaner. Clean a small area with a lightly damp cloth, then dry it. Odie's also lists a 1% mix for light-duty wipe downs on its newer All-In-One concentrate page; follow the label for the exact bottle you buy. Clean water is the manufacturer's requirement; distilled water is optional if you prefer it for mixing. Do not flood wood floors. Read Odie's cleaning FAQ.

Odie's Universal Oil is a finish, not a daily floor cleaner. Its floor instructions discuss occasional spot touch-ups after cleaning, with all excess oil wiped away. Follow the product and floor instructions for application and curing. Oil-soaked rags require the maker's fire-safe disposal precautions. Read Odie's wood-floor instructions.

Floors finished with Rubio Monocoat Oil Plus 2C

Rubio recommends plain water, Rubio Monocoat Soap, or Surface Care Spray for its interior oil-finished wood. Its Soap is a concentrate used to make a Surface Care cleaning mix at 1 part Soap to 100 parts water; the maker also gives ½ tablespoon in a 750 mL spray bottle. Lightly damp clean, remove loosened dirt, and allow the floor to dry. Rubio advises against using Soap or Surface Care more than once a week. Check Rubio's mixing directions and floor-care guide.

Do not substitute Odie's cleaner, vinegar, or a general-purpose “natural” cleaner for Rubio's specified care system on a Rubio finished floor. Natural ingredients alone do not tell you whether a product is compatible with a particular finish.

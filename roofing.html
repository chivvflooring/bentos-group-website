From structural framing to a dried-in home

Roofing is one part of a connected building envelope. For new homes, additions, and major renovations, the roof structure, decking, flashing, underlayment, penetrations, drainage, siding, and exterior finishes must be coordinated around the approved design and project scope.

  1. Review the property, plans, damage, or proposed construction.
  2. Define the roofing scope, material direction, responsibilities, and site conditions.
  3. Coordinate qualified professionals and schedule the work.
  4. Complete project-specific inspections and final walkthrough as applicable.

Roofing questions

Do you handle both replacement and new-construction roofing?

Yes. Submit the project details so we can review whether the need is a stand-alone replacement or roofing integrated with a larger construction scope.

Can you provide a price from a photo?

Photos help with the initial review, but a reliable proposal may require measurements, material selections, access review, and an on-site assessment.

Which areas do you serve?

We review projects across Metro and North Atlanta, including Alpharetta, Johns Creek, Milton, Roswell, Cumming, Sandy Springs, Dunwoody, Atlanta, Marietta, Woodstock, and Acworth. Coverage depends on project scope.

Cabinet renovation questions

Do I need to renovate the entire kitchen?

No. You can start with cabinets alone. Tell us what you want to keep and what needs to change so we can discuss a focused scope or related work if needed.

Should I paint my cabinets or replace them?

A finish change and a storage or layout problem are different goals. The material, existing coating, condition of the cabinet boxes and doors, and your desired layout guide the discussion. Photos are a starting point; suitability is confirmed after assessment.

Can you combine cabinets with hardwood flooring and painting?

Yes. Include the rooms, surfaces and flooring work you want in the request. We can discuss sequencing, protection and the separate items to include in the proposal.

How much will the cabinet project cost?

Cabinet quantity, materials, condition, preparation, finish, installation and related trade work affect the price. Call, text or request an estimate with your city and goals to begin a project-specific review.

Do I need measurements before contacting you?

No. Approximate sizes help if you have them, but your city, project description and photos are enough to start the conversation. Measurements can be arranged as the scope develops.

FAQ

Bathroom Remodeling Questions

What does a bathroom remodel in Johns Creek include?

A bathroom remodel can include showers, tubs, vanities, countertops, tile, flooring, lighting, fixtures, storage and other construction or finish work depending on the project's scope.

Can you remodel a small bathroom in Johns Creek?

Yes. Smaller bathrooms can often benefit significantly from better layouts, storage, lighting, vanity selection, shower configurations and carefully chosen finishes.

Can a bathroom remodel include a new shower?

Yes. Bathroom remodeling can include shower replacement or remodeling, including tile, shower surfaces, fixtures, glass and related construction when appropriate.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost in Johns Creek?

Bathroom remodeling costs vary according to the size of the bathroom, materials, fixtures, cabinetry, tile, plumbing, electrical work and the extent of construction. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic price.

Can you remodel a primary bathroom?

Yes. Primary bathroom renovations can be planned around improved shower space, storage, vanities, flooring, lighting, fixtures and other features that make the room more functional for everyday use.

How do I get started with a bathroom remodel in Johns Creek?

Contact Bento's Group with a description of the bathroom, the improvements you want and photos of the existing space if available. The project can then be discussed and scoped around your goals.

FAQ

Full Home Renovation Questions

What does a full home renovation include?

A full home renovation can include multiple areas of the property, such as kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, living spaces, finishes, lighting and other construction or remodeling work depending on the project's scope.

Can you renovate multiple rooms at the same time?

Yes. A whole-home renovation can coordinate work across multiple rooms so that materials, construction, finishes and scheduling are planned as one larger project.

Can a full home renovation include a kitchen and bathroom remodel?

Yes. Kitchen and bathroom remodeling can be included as part of a broader home renovation when the project calls for improvements throughout multiple areas of the property.

How much does a full home renovation cost in Cumming?

The cost of a full home renovation depends heavily on the size of the property, number of rooms, materials, structural changes, plumbing, electrical work and overall scope. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic price.

How long does a whole-home renovation take?

Project duration varies according to the home's size, scope of work, materials, construction requirements and project sequencing. A more accurate timeline can be established after the renovation is evaluated and scoped.

Can you renovate an older home?

Yes. Renovating an existing home can involve updating outdated finishes, kitchens, bathrooms, flooring, layouts and other areas while accounting for the property's existing conditions.

How do I start a full home renovation?

Contact Bento's Group with information about your home, the areas you want to renovate and the improvements you have in mind. The project can then be discussed and scoped around your priorities.

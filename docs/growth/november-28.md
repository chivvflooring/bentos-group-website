## Operating the referral program

- After a completed job and the final walkthrough, ask an interested customer if they know someone who could use the same work. Share the customer section of the referral page. Let the new household initiate contact. Keep review requests independent of referrals and incentives.
- Share the real estate section with an agent after they request a partner conversation. Ask for city, scope, property access constraints and the date photography or a listing is planned. Start with paint and punch-list items. Confirm availability before offering deadlines. The page provides both photo intake and a traditional request.
- For a neighborhood group with permission to post, lead with one relevant project example and the tagged photo link. Identify the real service area. Use only an owner-approved original photograph, caption and location.
- At first human contact, record when the inquiry arrived, confirm acquisition source and whether it is a repeat project, and qualify service, geography, timing and scope. Record the estimate and signed outcome only after they occur. Public campaign labels support investigation and can be shared between channels.
- For small painting work, group related rooms or touch-ups when the homeowner wants them. Scope protection, surface preparation, materials, travel, labor and cleanup before pricing. The estimator checks direct job costs and the owner’s margin floor before committing an estimate. Use the same review for larger basements, kitchens and baths, with trade and scope assumptions written down.
- At job closeout, enter earned job revenue and actual direct job costs from the books after reconciliation. Cash collected comes from separate payment records. Gross profit is the difference between earned job revenue and direct cost for reconciled jobs. This operational view is grouped by first dashboard closeout entry date, which survives corrections. The accounting ledger supplies financial-period reporting. No closeout data means profit remains unknown.

The dashboard counts new staff-classified discovery from organic web search, Maps and AI separately in its channel breakdown and together for the organic goals of 20 qualified leads and four signed contracts.

# Sales follow-up in the existing pipeline

Use the existing private Lead Follow-up sheet for offline and follow-up records. For website inquiries, use the authenticated sales dashboard after activation and reference its lead ID in the sheet only when someone needs an offline follow-up. Keep one private project ID and link overlaps. Keep completed records, customer information and costs outside this public repository.

| Opportunity | Staff owner and next action |
| --- | --- |
| New homeowner inquiry | Intake owner records received and first human response times, confirms city, service, scope, timing and contact preference, then assigns an estimator. |
| Real estate partnership | Partnerships owner records brokerage or agent, referral permission, property and key dates. Ask for listing or inspection priorities and offer the painting and repair photo route. |
| Neighborhood referral | Intake owner records the confirmed referring source and relationship privately, then qualifies the project using the same homeowner criteria. |
| School sponsorship referral | Partnerships owner records the school or event as the source only when confirmed. Accept an adult's direct inquiry. Use a school-approved placement and track sponsorship costs separately after approval. |
| Outstanding estimate | Estimator sets the follow-up date when sending the estimate, records the customer's decision or objection, and offers revised scope after confirming interest. Respect stop requests. |
| Previous customer | Account owner records an approved contact basis and checks the suppression list. Record repeat-project status, prior project ID and the new scope. |
| Potential HomeCare | Service owner records requested tasks and cadence, estimates travel, labor and materials, and tests a one-off Punch List first. Record pilot interest privately while membership terms and economics are unconfirmed. |

During business hours, assign new inquiries the same business day as an internal target. Each open row has one owner, a next action and a due date. Use received timestamp, first human response, qualified date, estimate sent/date/value, signed date/value, collected revenue, estimated direct job costs, and actual direct job costs from the estimating or accounting source. Response time is first human response minus receipt. Gross profit is recognized revenue minus corresponding direct job costs for the same scope and period. Keep projected gross profit and realized gross profit separate. Leave missing cost, date and revenue data blank. Review overdue items and acquisition sources weekly with the sales lead. Treat email permission, customer response permission and sponsor approval as separate fields.

FAQ

Kitchen Remodeling Questions

What does a kitchen remodel in Roswell include?

A kitchen remodel can include cabinets, countertops, islands, flooring, backsplashes, lighting, fixtures, layout changes and other construction or finish work depending on the project's scope.

Can you remodel an existing kitchen without changing the entire layout?

Yes. A kitchen can often be significantly improved without a complete layout change. The appropriate approach depends on the existing cabinets, utilities, available space and goals for the renovation.

Can a kitchen remodel include a kitchen island?

Yes. A kitchen island can be incorporated when the available space and layout allow it. Islands can provide additional workspace, storage and seating.

How much does kitchen remodeling cost in Roswell?

Kitchen remodeling costs vary according to the size of the kitchen, cabinets, countertops, appliances, flooring, layout changes, electrical and plumbing work and other project requirements. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic price.

Can you help with a complete kitchen renovation?

Yes. A complete kitchen renovation can coordinate multiple parts of the space, including cabinetry, countertops, flooring, lighting, fixtures, finishes and construction work according to the project's scope.

How do I get started with a kitchen remodel?

Contact Bento's Group with a description of your kitchen, the improvements you want and photos of the existing space if available. The project can then be discussed and scoped around your goals.

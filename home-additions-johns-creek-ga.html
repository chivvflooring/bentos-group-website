FAQ

Home Addition Questions

What types of home additions can you build in Johns Creek?

Home additions can include bedrooms, bathrooms, family rooms, living areas, home offices and other custom spaces depending on the property and project requirements.

Is a home addition better than moving?

For some homeowners, adding space to an existing property can be a practical alternative to moving. The right decision depends on the home, desired space, budget and long-term plans.

Can you add a bedroom to an existing home?

Yes. Bedroom additions can be planned as part of a larger home expansion, subject to the property's layout, construction requirements and applicable permitting.

Can a home addition include a bathroom?

Yes. An addition can incorporate a new bathroom when the property's layout and project requirements allow it. Plumbing, electrical and other construction requirements are evaluated as part of the project scope.

How much does a home addition cost in Johns Creek?

The cost depends on the size of the addition, foundation, structural work, utilities, finishes, design requirements and other project-specific factors. A project-specific estimate is more useful than a generic price.

How do I start planning a home addition?

Start by discussing the space you need, the existing home and your goals with Bento's Group. The project can then be evaluated and scoped around the property's conditions and your priorities.

Choose the painting scope

Interior rooms

List walls, ceilings, trim and doors room by room. Include nail holes, drywall patches, stains, accent walls and whether the home will be occupied during the work.

Exterior surfaces

Identify siding, fascia, soffits, window trim, doors and other surfaces. Cleaning, scraping, caulking, primer and repairs depend on the existing condition and selected coating.

Cabinet painting

Share the cabinet material, existing finish, door condition and desired color. Cabinet preparation and coating compatibility need review; replacement cabinetry and layout changes are separate scopes.

Questions before requesting an estimate

What should the estimate include?

It should identify the included surfaces, preparation, repairs, primer, paint products, protection, cleanup and exclusions. A project-specific scope is more useful than a price based only on square footage.

Can painting follow flooring or remodeling?

Yes. Include related flooring, cabinet or renovation work in the request so surface protection and the work sequence can be discussed together.

What about HOA requirements?

Confirm current work-hour, access and color-approval requirements with the applicable community or HOA before materials are ordered. Share those requirements during planning.

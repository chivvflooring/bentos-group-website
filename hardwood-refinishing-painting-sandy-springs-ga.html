Information that makes the first call useful

  1. Property status: already owned, under contract or still considering a purchase.
  2. Access: when a site review is possible and whether the home will be empty.
  3. Scope: rooms, approximate area, stairs, walls, ceilings, trim, cabinets or bathrooms.
  4. Timing: expected closing, move-in or other deadline. A requested date is reviewed before any schedule is confirmed.
  5. Photos: wide views plus close-ups of floor damage, wall repairs and important transitions.

Questions from Sandy Springs homeowners and buyers

Can hardwood refinishing and painting be planned before move-in?

Yes. Share the expected closing or access date, affected rooms and current conditions. The work sequence, property access and final schedule must be confirmed after review.

Can I get a final estimate from listing photos?

Listing photos can start the conversation, but they cannot confirm measurements, floor condition, preparation or a final price. A project-specific estimate requires reliable scope information and may require access to the property.

Should floors or painting happen first?

The order depends on the floor process, wall and trim repairs, finish products and protection plan. Include both services in one request so the sequence can be discussed before work is scheduled.

Can kitchen or bathroom work be included?

Yes. Include every room you expect to change so demolition, trade work, flooring and painting can be planned in a practical order. Licensed professionals participate where the agreed scope requires them.
